## Original inventory and exact coordinates

The private derivation re-enumerates complete original records through owning
validators, independently reconstructs request-rooted reachability and compares
the complete manifest entries and retained record closure, including its usage.
Request reference, scope, semantic time, parent hashes, original receipts, selector
outcomes and protocol candidate lists remain bound. Missing, extra, reordered or
substituted original entries/frontiers fail before dependency coordinates return.

`ruleDependencies.sources` retains every derived manifest observation, including
unused and unreadable members. `frontier` retains every original artifact, trace,
declaration and unresolved-selector occurrence. Source indexes refer to this exact
sorted inventory; edge indexes refer to this invocation's original graph. Frontier
indexes refer to this report's full original frontier. They are not reusable caller
IDs or permission to fetch replacement evidence.

The separate `rootContext` expands non-rule policy dependencies and candidate
context. Each rule keeps all its original policy predicate/source edges. Artifact
rules also include the selected candidate component occurrence. Comparison rules
include the **complete candidate comparison inventory**, including unreadable and
unused members needed by the owning unique/ambiguous/unresolved selection boundary.
Assessment rules include their exact candidate declaration when present; absence
does not cause another assessment to be selected. Approval declarations remain
`not_evaluated` with `approval_not_evaluated` in the owning ordered inputs.

Traversal follows every retained protocol sibling even when the single target is
null. Future/unavailable and descriptor-mismatching retained members are not dropped
to choose a winner. Joined declarations keep their authority frontier. Shared
records are unique within a frame; all original repeated edges/frontiers remain,
and separate rules retain separate frames. Encountering a request root again does
not expand all other rules into that rule's evidence dependencies.

## Admission and meaning

Independent whole-record enumeration, canonical context, every manifest/frontier
entry and complete root/rule frames share the original finite count/UTF-8 budget.
Frame counts include repeated seed/source/edge/frontier coordinates. Returned data
owns nested copies; this pure join performs no new repository/object/key I/O.

Coordinates describe captured dependencies. They do not qualify retained values,
decide applicability or predicate outcomes, authenticate a live registry, prove
loaded code, grant approval or seal a snapshot. In particular, root context and
unused inventory must not turn an unrelated missing member into an invented zero
or a rule result. Source authority, owning semantic checks and exact missingness
still need their explicit interpretation before bounded snapshot/result contracts.
The existing read-only recheck transaction ends before reporting; publication
requires complete guards and execution fences in one still-open transaction.

Real encrypted-memory capture and native held-metadata composition cover original
coordinates, transitive fixture/predecessor paths, repeated frontiers, missing
comparison members, future protocol siblings, mutations and exact count/byte limits.
Full uncached checks and exact-commit CI/security precede dependent implementation.
Sealed contracts/publication, all seven predicates, durable jobs/worker authority,
API/SDK/recovery/contributor acceptance and independent exit audit remain open.
