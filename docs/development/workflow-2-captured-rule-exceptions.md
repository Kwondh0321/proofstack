## Fixed original subjects and complete histories

`resolveCapturedRuleExceptionQuorum` accepts one complete exact rule-exception census, original
actor bindings, installed review context, captured cut and all original control observations.
The fixed owning server must derive the subject, actual policy rule and reviewer requirement;
callers cannot choose a favorable policy list, latest approval, computed label or validator.
The function does not create a default exception authority or prove the supplied context's origin.

The shared fixed census engine keeps the normal wrapper's API, error class and result shape.
Exception records retain their original kind and canonical digest. Every action, opposing/escalating/
abstaining row, expiry, revocation, exact supersession, competing successor and current authority/
identity observation remains in the result. A missing actor, invalid physical source preimage or
unknown provenance cannot disappear through favorable sampling. Candidate and policy authors and
actual subject evaluators cannot support their own exception. Unknown evaluator provenance holds.

The existing matcher assigns distinct eligible principals to eligible groups with required human
witnesses. Multiple memberships never multiply people. Both operations retain the 4,096-actor
admission. Natural expiry of an original login does not itself revoke an already recorded review;
actual session/grant/authority revocation and approval expiry still apply. This is not authentication
of a new action, proof of human expertise or a way to freshen its original receipt.

## Conditions and every control occurrence

The exception report includes the [per-approval control inspections](workflow-2-captured-exception-controls.md).
Missing any known occurrence makes the whole census unavailable, including a missing inactive
record's observation. Explicit skipped inactive controls must retain an actual expiry/revocation/
supersession fact; a supporting action cannot pretend to be a non-approval. An active affirmative
action requires retained content/native-catalog observations. Uninspected, unverified, changed,
expired or unavailable controls remain explicit and cannot support a successful exception.

Shared physical catalog preimages must agree across all approvals. Two individually consistent
reports with contradictory current catalogs are unavailable together. Unknown conditions/conflicts
and active opposition/escalation retain their original conservative precedence. The earliest actual
control expiry is included with action and reviewer-authority expiry for selected witnesses.
Retained bytes do not automatically prove a compensating control's effectiveness.

## One original admission and later publication

The owning census admits each original approval and whole control observation once, before deep
control parsing/grouping. The fixed internal control pass revalidates the canonical definition and
admits retained content/catalog projections on that same meter, avoiding a second approval or whole
observation charge for pure validation. The standalone inspector still admits its own original rows.
Neither entry point exposes a public skip flag or arbitrary callback. The owning server
must supply the original request-owned meter and charge every actual failed/discarded/repeated/
relationship read too; class shape or a detached usage report cannot prove job binding or authority.

The result is eligibility and captured evidence only. The existing classifier preserves the original
known violation when an exact waivable exception applies; a non-waivable violation still rejects,
indeterminate evidence stays unresolved, withdrawal stays unavailable and approval_required still
needs its distinct normal approval. Original evaluation outcomes remain unchanged. The future fixed
composer must validate complete original/current sources, actual OIDC/CSRF or separate publisher
proof, installed authority, native cut/epoch and all required guards through its atomic write.
No public result POST, native role/grant, API/SDK, worker or deployment is added here.

## Verification boundaries

[Exception cases](../../packages/core/src/release/captured-rule-exception-quorum.test.ts) cover current
authority/identity, author/evaluator exclusions, opposition/conditions, exact history/forks, natural
login expiry, action expiry, every control failure, forged contexts/references, shared physical
conflicts, 4,096 complete rows/controls and final human witness, exact/one-below admission and the
existing conservative rule classifier. Normal and control-inspection regressions run alongside them.
The large exception case uses the unchanged normal large-case budget of 30 seconds; no existing
assertion, payload count, coverage threshold or product deadline is relaxed. Synthetic public
preimages do not establish real-provider authority or checkpoint acceptance.
