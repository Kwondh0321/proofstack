# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ProofStack contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to making participation safe,
respectful, and useful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or
point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while remaining precise about risk.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the boundaries in `SECURITY.md`.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and avoid repeating harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose credentials, customer data, prompts, telemetry, or private reports.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional, security-sensitive project.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, events, and private
project communications. It also applies when someone officially represents ProofStack elsewhere.

## Reporting

Do not include names, screenshots, or sensitive details in a public issue. While the project is in
its pre-release bootstrap phase, use GitHub's private reporting form at
<https://github.com/Kwondh0321/proofstack/security/advisories/new> and prefix the summary with
`Conduct:`. The private form is used as a temporary confidential channel for both security and
conduct reports.

Maintainers will limit access to people needed to review the report, seek consent before sharing
identifying details, and communicate next steps as soon as practical. A knowingly false or
retaliatory report is itself a violation; an unproven good-faith report is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, restrict participation, impose a
temporary suspension, or permanently ban a participant. The response will be proportionate to the
behavior, its impact, prior incidents, and whether harm is acknowledged and repaired.

Maintainers must recuse themselves from a report in which they are directly involved. If no
uninvolved maintainer is available, the project will identify an independent reviewer before
taking any non-urgent final action. Immediate protective action may still be taken when needed to
prevent continuing harm.

## Appeals

A person subject to an enforcement action may request one review through the same private channel.
The appeal should identify a factual or procedural error rather than repeat the original argument.
The appeal decision is final for that incident.
